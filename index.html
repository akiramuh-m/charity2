OUR STORY

Rooted in community. Focused on possibility.

Founded by Ugandan educators and community advocates, Save the Child Uganda works so children can thrive with the support of the people around them.

About us

We believe progress starts by listening.

Save the Child Uganda partners with caregivers, teachers and local leaders to support children’s learning, wellbeing and safety.

Our mission To help children and families build safe, healthy and hopeful futures through community-led support.
Our valuesDignity · Inclusion · Care · Accountability · Partnership
LeadershipExecutive Director; Programs Lead.
GovernanceGoverned by an independent board.
Our work

Practical support, shaped with local communities.

We co-create programs directly with residents, local leaders, and grassroots groups.

Learning Together

Community reading circles, caregiver sessions and learning materials for children ages 6–12. Location: Wakiso District.

Healthy Starts

Caregiver-led wellbeing sessions and referrals to local services. Locations: Mukono and Jinja districts.

Safe Spaces

Community activities that help children feel included and supported. Reach: four community centers in Central Uganda.
