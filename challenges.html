Our Challenges

Challenges we face.

Like many community-led organisations, we work with limited resources against growing need. These are the challenges we navigate every day.

  • Limited funding — demand for our programs outpaces available resources, making it hard to reach every child who needs support.
  • Reaching remote communities — some of the most underserved families live in hard-to-reach areas, limiting consistent access to our reading circles and safe spaces.
  • Recruiting and retaining volunteers — training and keeping committed volunteers and community facilitators across three districts requires ongoing investment.
  • Measuring long-term impact — tracking how children and caregivers fare over time across dispersed communities is complex with limited monitoring capacity.
  • Balancing growth with sustainability — expanding to new communities must be weighed against our ability to maintain quality and continue supporting existing programs.
How we respond

What we’re doing about it.

We don’t ignore these challenges — we work with communities to address them.

Community fundraisingWe raise support locally and through partners to keep programs running without depending on a single source.
Volunteer trainingWe invest in training community facilitators so programs continue even as volunteers move on.
Simple monitoringWe use practical, low-cost tools to track attendance, feedback and learning progress across districts.
Phased growthWe expand only when we are confident we can maintain quality in the communities we already serve.
