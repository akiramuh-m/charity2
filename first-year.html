Our programs

What we run.

Community reading circles

Weekly reading circles for children ages 6–12 in Wakiso, Mukono and Jinja districts, giving children a regular place to learn together.

Caregiver wellbeing sessions

Caregiver-led sessions that help parents support their children’s learning at home, with referrals to local services where needed.

Safe space activities

Community center activities that help children feel included, supported and safe across Central Uganda.

Looking ahead

Year two and beyond.

Building on what we’ve learned, we plan to grow responsibly and keep quality at the center.

Expand reading circlesReach more communities in Wakiso, Mukono and Jinja, and consider new districts where need is high.
Strengthen caregiver supportDeepen caregiver wellbeing sessions and add more local service referrals.
