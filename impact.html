What this means

Behind the numbers.

Each figure represents real children, caregivers and communities whose lives are changing.

420 childrenAttended weekly reading circles, gaining a regular place to learn, read and grow with their peers.
18 reading groupsRun monthly across three districts, bringing learning support closer to home for families.
76% of caregiversReported improved access to learning support for their children after attending caregiver wellbeing sessions.
Four community centersHost safe space activities where children feel included, supported and protected.
