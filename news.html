News & updates

Latest from our communities.

Recent news, milestones and announcements from Save the Child Uganda.

Reading circles reach three districts

March 2026 — Community reading circles now run weekly across Wakiso, Mukono and Jinja, giving children a regular place to learn together.

First community learning day

February 2026 — Families, caregivers and volunteers gathered in Wakiso for a community reading day, bringing learning support to more homes.

New reading groups open

January 2026 — Reading circles opened across Mukono and Jinja, expanding access to learning support for more children.

Caregiver wellbeing sessions launch

November 2025 — Caregiver-led sessions began helping parents support their children’s learning at home, with referrals to local services.

Four community centers active

October 2025 — Community centers in Central Uganda now host safe space activities where children feel included and supported.

Save the Child Uganda founded

2025 — Founded by Ugandan educators and community advocates who believe children thrive when surrounded by support.
