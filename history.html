Our History

Our story so far.

Save the Child Uganda was founded by Ugandan educators and community advocates who believe children do best when the people around them have the tools and support to help them thrive.

FoundedSave the Child Uganda began with a shared belief in community-led support for children’s learning, wellbeing and safety.
First programsLaunched community reading circles, caregiver wellbeing sessions and safe space activities in Wakiso District.
GrowthExpanded to Mukono and Jinja districts, with programs hosted at four community centers in Central Uganda.
TodayOperating for one year, supporting children and families across three districts through community-led programs.
Timeline

Key milestones.

2025Save the Child Uganda founded by Ugandan educators and community advocates. First community reading circles launched in Wakiso District.
Early 2025Programs Lead joins the team. Caregiver wellbeing sessions begin, helping parents support their children’s learning at home.
Mid 2025Expansion to Mukono and Jinja districts. Four community centers established to host safe space activities.
Late 2025Monitoring & Evaluation Officer joins. Structured impact tracking begins across all supported communities.
2026Reading circles reach hundreds of children across three districts. 76% of participating caregivers report improved access to learning support.
Why community-led

Our approach.

We believe lasting change happens when communities lead their own development.

Save the Child Uganda was started by Ugandans who saw a need in their own neighborhoods. Rather than bringing outside solutions, we listen to caregivers, teachers and local leaders, then build programs that fit the realities of each community. This means our work is culturally relevant, sustainable, and owned by the people it serves.
