OUR STORY

Rooted in community. Focused on possibility.

Save the Child Uganda began with a shared belief: children do best when the people around them have the tools and support to help them thrive. Working alongside Ugandan educators and community advocates, we partner with caregivers, teachers and local leaders to support children’s learning, wellbeing and safety.

Our Profile

Who we are.

Save the Child Uganda is a community-led organisation working across Wakiso, Mukono and Jinja districts to help children access learning, care and opportunities for a brighter future.

We believe progress starts by listening — to children, to caregivers, to teachers and to local leaders. Our work is shaped by the communities we serve, not imposed from the outside.

In detail

Mission, vision, objectives, goals, leadership and governance.

A closer look at what guides Save the Child Uganda and how we are run.

Our missionTo help children and families build safe, healthy and hopeful futures through community-led support. We partner with caregivers, teachers and local leaders so children can access learning, care and opportunities for a brighter future.
Our visionA Uganda where every child, regardless of background or circumstance, has access to quality learning, care and the opportunity to thrive in a safe and supportive community.
Our objectivesImprove children’s learning outcomes through reading circles and caregiver wellbeing sessions. Strengthen caregiver and community support networks. Create safe spaces where children can play, learn and grow across our supported districts.
Our goalsReach more children across Wakiso, Mukono and Jinja districts. Expand reading circles and caregiver sessions to additional communities. Build sustainable, community-led programs that continue beyond our direct involvement.
LeadershipThe Executive Director leads the organisation’s vision, strategy and partnerships. The Programs Lead oversees reading circles, caregiver sessions and safe space activities at community centers. The Monitoring & Evaluation Officer tracks program outcomes and reports on impact across all supported communities.
GovernanceSave the Child Uganda is governed by an independent board that sets strategy, oversees finances and ensures accountability. We are accountable to the children, caregivers and communities we serve, and operate transparently with community-led decision-making.
Our values

What we stand for.

These values guide every decision we make and every program we run.

DignityEvery child and family we work with is treated with respect and worth.
InclusionNo child is left behind because of where they live, their ability, or their family’s circumstances.
CareWe put children’s wellbeing and safety at the center of everything we do.
AccountabilityWe are answerable to the children, caregivers and communities we serve.
PartnershipWe work hand-in-hand with local leaders, teachers and families — we do not work alone.
