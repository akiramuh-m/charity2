Our work

Practical support, shaped with local communities.

We co-create programs directly with residents, local leaders, and grassroots groups.

▤

Learning Together

Community reading circles, caregiver sessions and learning materials for children ages 6–12. Location: Wakiso District.

♡

Healthy Starts

Caregiver-led wellbeing sessions and referrals to local services. Locations: Mukono and Jinja districts.

⌂

Safe Spaces

Community activities that help children feel included and supported. Reach: four community centers in Central Uganda.
