Our work

Practical support, shaped with local communities.

We co-create programs directly with residents, local leaders, and grassroots groups across Wakiso, Mukono and Jinja districts.

Program details

How our programs work.

Each program is shaped with the communities we serve, with activities designed around local needs and resources.

Learning Together

Weekly reading circles give children ages 6–12 a regular place to read, learn and grow with support from trained volunteer facilitators.

Healthy Starts

Caregiver-led wellbeing sessions help parents support their children’s health and learning at home, with referrals to local health services.

Safe Spaces

Community centers provide supervised activities where children can play, learn and connect with peers in a safe environment.
